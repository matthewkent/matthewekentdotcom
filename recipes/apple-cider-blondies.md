## Ingredients

### Apple cider reduction
- 64oz apple cider

### Blondies
- 100g apple cider reduction
- 4 oz melted butter
- 200g brown sugar
- 1 egg + 1 egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 120g all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon

### Topping
- 50g granulated sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Method

### Reduction
1. Heat apple cider in a wide saucepan and simmer until reduced to 1/4 of original volume, about 45 minutes.

### Blondies
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line an 8-inch square aluminum pan with parchment paper.
2. Mix wet ingredients in a large bowl until combined.
3. Add dry ingredients and mix until combined.
4. Sprinkle topping over the batter.
5. Bake until a toothpick tester comes out clean, about 20 to 25 minutes.

## Notes

64oz of cider will yield about 400g of reduction, enough for 4 batches. Scale the reduction as desired if you have more or less cider.
